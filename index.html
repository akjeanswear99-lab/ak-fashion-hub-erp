From the Products screen, tapping 🏷 on a product automatically queues only the stock that hasn't been printed yet — e.g. click a product with sizes 30 (stock 2) and 32 (stock 3), and 2 and 3 tags come up ready to print. Anything already printed earlier is remembered and skipped, so re-clicking only pulls in newly added stock.
